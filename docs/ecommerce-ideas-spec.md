# Indizio Ecommerce Ideas — Living Product Specification

**Status:** Planned for later; intentionally excluded from the launch-facing website  
**Last updated:** August 22, 2026

## 1. Purpose

Ecommerce Ideas is a practical editorial library of concepts, experiments, and execution details that operators can test in their own stores. It complements CRO Fieldnotes: Fieldnotes explains researched evidence, while Ecommerce Ideas translates useful observations into focused opportunities.

## 2. Distinction from CRO Fieldnotes

### CRO Fieldnotes

- Research-first
- Longer analysis
- Brand and industry evidence
- Teardowns and blueprints

### Ecommerce Ideas

- Application-first
- Concise and actionable
- One clear opportunity per article
- Includes hypothesis, implementation guidance, and measurement

An idea can originate from a Fieldnote, but it should remain a separate content record with its own user intent and URL.

## 3. Idea formats

- CRO experiment
- Merchandising concept
- Trust-building improvement
- Product-page enhancement
- Retention or post-purchase idea
- Navigation and discovery improvement
- Operational or content tactic

## 4. Index page

The future `/ideas` page should provide an editorial list or compact card system containing:

- Clear, outcome-oriented title
- Short explanation
- Relevant industry
- Funnel stage or surface
- Effort and expected impact indicators when responsibly supportable
- Link to the full idea

Filtering should eventually support industry, funnel stage, store surface, effort, and evidence level.

## 5. Idea page structure

Each idea should answer:

1. What is the opportunity?
2. What customer problem might it solve?
3. Where was the signal observed?
4. How could a store implement it?
5. What should be measured?
6. What risks or confounding factors exist?

Recommended page sections:

- Summary
- Evidence or inspiration
- Hypothesis
- Implementation blueprint
- Measurement plan
- Variations
- Related websites and Fieldnotes

Ideas should not make unsupported conversion claims or present correlation as causation.

## 6. Payload content model

- Title and slug
- Short description
- Rich content
- Industry
- Funnel stage
- Store surface
- Idea type
- Effort level
- Evidence level
- Related websites
- Related Fieldnotes
- Status, publication date, and featured rank
- SEO fields

## 7. Saving

Users should eventually save ideas independently of websites and articles. Ideas may use private collections, but their records and public counts must remain distinguishable from website saves.

## 8. SEO strategy

Target practical queries only when the article genuinely answers them, such as ecommerce product-page ideas, cart-drawer improvements, trust-signal examples, and retention experiments. Each indexed idea must provide original context and a usable implementation plan.

## 9. Success metrics

- Organic visits to idea pages
- Idea saves
- Related Fieldnote and website engagement
- Newsletter subscriptions
- Return visits
- Qualitative feedback from operators who tested an idea

## 10. Release plan

1. Finalize taxonomy and page template.
2. Publish a small set of strong ideas sourced from existing research.
3. Connect each idea to evidence in the Website Library or Fieldnotes.
4. Add saving and collections.
5. Add filters when catalogue size justifies them.
